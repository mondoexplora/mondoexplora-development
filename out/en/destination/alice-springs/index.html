Family-Friendly Alice Springs Outback Oasis near Lasseters

Alice Springs, Australia

An outback oasis in the heart of Australia’s vast Red Centre, Crowne Plaza Alice Springs Lasserters, an IHG Hotel, offers family-friendly convenience under the shadow of the mighty MacDonnell Ranges. With an unrivalled location - adjacent to the Alice Springs Convention Centre and entertainment hub Lasseters and only minutes from the CBD and airport - the region's countless treasures are within easy reach, elevated by stunning mountain views. Swim your way through Tjoritja West MacDonnell National Park's many permanent waterholes, wander through Alice Springs' world-class Indigenous museums and galleries or admire the scenery by camel or hot air balloon. Between walkabouts in the great red beyond, sip cold ones by the water at Splash Poolside Café, rebalance and revitalise with a visit to onsite spa Lavish or savour mouthwatering wagyu at Juicy Rump.
