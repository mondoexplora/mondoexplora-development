Puerto Vallarta, Mexico

On the northern edge of Banderas Bay near the historic centre of Puerto Vallarta lies a secluded gem that encapsulates the essence of Mexico - the luxurious five-star Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit. Designed with grandiose architecture and surrounded by stunning tropical landscapes, the award-winning resort features six restaurants showcasing the best culinary delights from around the world.
