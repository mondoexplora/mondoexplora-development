Glamorous Ski-In, Ski-Out Montana Mountain Retreat with 10,000-Square-Foot Spa

Montana, United States

The natural beauty of Montana's Big Sky Country is front and centre at the area's first five-star resort, Montage Big Sky, a majestic ski-in/ski-out mountain retreat. During winter, the mountains are dusted with a thick blanket of snow, and in summer, the resort offers recreational activities including a private 18-hole golf course designed by Tom Weiskopf. The 10,000-square-foot Spa Montage promises a transcendent pampering experience, with alpine-inspired treatments and heated plunge pools. Onsite dining is a celebration of culinary excellence: savour pre-dinner cocktails with live piano music at Alpenglow, dine on traditional Northern Italian cuisine at Cortina and feast on wood-fired pizzas between bowling rounds at the fun-filled Beartooth Pub & Rec.
