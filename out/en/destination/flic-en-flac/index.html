Tropical Mauritius Beachfront Retreat with Eight Restaurants & Bars & Award-Winning Spa

Flic En Flac, Mauritius

Nestled along Mauritius’ tranquil west coast is a tropical sanctuary where French Zest meets the local culture, all with a backdrop of lush gardens, powder sands and crystal-clear water. A stay at Sofitel Mauritius L'Impérial Resort & Spa is a masterclass in escapism -indulge in five exquisite restaurants and three stylish bars, or rejuvenate your senses at the award-winning Sofitel Spa while your kids stay entertained at the Kids' Club with its very own pool. Leave your patch of paradise to explore the vibrant local markets of Port Louis, renowned for their colourful textiles, handcrafted jewellery and aromatic spices. For the nature enthusiasts, the Pamplemousses Botanical Garden, home to a myriad of exotic plants and rare bird species, is a must-visit. Bask in breathtaking sunsets on the pristine beach, swim among vibrant marine life, or simply unwind in a private sanctuary of relaxation.
