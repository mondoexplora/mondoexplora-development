Asia's Natural Wonders: 5 Must‑See Places

From Ha Long Bay to Kelimutu's color‑shifting lakes, explore Asia's most spectacular natural sights and where to stay nearby.

By MondoExplora Editors•October 20, 2025•Featured
AsiaNatureUNESCOIslandsMountains

Ha Long Bay, Vietnam

Ha Long Bay is a UNESCO gem of 1,600+ limestone karsts rising from emerald water. Explore caves like Sung Sot, kayak hidden lagoons, and catch sunset from Ti Top Island.

  • Vinpearl Resort & Spa Ha Long (private‑island luxury)
  • Ha Long Boutique Hotel (central, near marina)
  • Overnight bay cruises (Indochina Sails, Paradise Elegance)

Zhangjiajie / Wulingyuan, China

Otherworldly sandstone pillars inspired Avatar’s Hallelujah Mountains. Don’t miss Tianzi Mountain views, the Bailong Elevator, and the Glass Bridge.

  • Pullman Zhangjiajie Hotel
  • Crowne Plaza Zhangjiajie Wulingyuan
  • No.5 Valley Inn (eco boutique)

Everest Base Camp, Nepal

The world’s highest peak anchors a trek of Sherpa villages, monasteries, and epic views of Lhotse and Ama Dablam. Base Camp sits at ~5,364 m.

  • Hotel Everest View (Namche Bazaar)
  • Yeti Mountain Home lodges (route network)

Kelimutu Crater Lakes, Indonesia

Three side‑by‑side volcanic lakes change color with minerals and gases—most magical at sunrise. A site of deep spiritual meaning for the Lio people.

  • Kelimutu Crater Lakes Ecolodge (Moni)
  • Bintang Lodge Moni
  • Hidayah Homestay

Puerto Princesa Underground River, Philippines

A vast limestone karst system and one of the world’s longest navigable underground rivers—explore caverns by boat within pristine rainforest.

  • Daluyon Beach & Mountain Resort
  • Sheridan Beach Resort & Spa
  • Floral Villarosa (city base)
