Asia's Man‑Made Marvels: Icons You Must See

From the Taj Mahal to Himeji Castle, explore Asia’s greatest human‑made landmarks with nearby stays.

By MondoExplora Editors•October 21, 2025
AsiaCultureArchitectureHistory

Taj Mahal, India

Pristine white marble, perfect symmetry, and serene Mughal gardens make sunrise at the Taj unforgettable.

  • Stay: The Oberoi Amarvilas (Agra)

Angkor Wat, Cambodia

Grand Khmer architecture intertwined with jungle. Soft dawn light over reflecting pools is pure magic.

  • Stay: Raffles Grand Hotel d’Angkor (Siem Reap)

Great Wall of China

Ancient engineering snakes across mountains. Golden hour paints towers and ridgelines in warm hues.

  • Stay: Commune by the Great Wall

Borobudur, Indonesia

World’s largest Buddhist monument; terraces of story‑filled reliefs and bell‑shaped stupas.

  • Stay: Amanjiwo

Himeji Castle, Japan

‘White Heron’ elegance meets superb preservation, manicured grounds, and panoramic top‑floor views.

  • Stay: Hotel Granvia Himeji
